Family Home privacy policy

Last updated: 28 October 2026

Who uses this app

Family Home is a private app for one family. It is not offered to the public. Only family members invited by the family administrator can sign in.

Where data is kept

All Family Home data is stored on the family's own computer. It is not sold, rented or shared with anyone outside the family. It is not used for advertising and it is not sent to any artificial intelligence service.

Google Calendar

Connecting Google Calendar is optional, and each person decides for their own Google account. If a person connects, Family Home asks Google for permission to:

  • see the list of their calendars, so they can choose which ones to show in the app;
  • create one calendar called "Family Home" and add, change and remove events in it;
  • see, add, change and remove events in calendars they own, so that edits made in either place stay in step.

Family Home uses this access only to keep that person's calendar and the app in step. Events from a person's Google Calendar are private to that person inside the app unless they choose to share one. Health related appointments are sent to Google with a general title only, unless the person in charge of that profile allows details.

The permission Google gives the app is stored encrypted on the family computer and is never shown to other family members. Family Home's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Stopping and removing data

A person can disconnect Google Calendar in the app at any time, or remove Family Home's access at myaccount.google.com/permissions. After disconnecting, the app stops reading and writing their calendar and can remove the copied events from the app. Their Google Calendar itself is left as it is.

Any family member can ask the family administrator to delete their data from Family Home.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to the family administrator at the support email shown on the Google sign in screen.
